On the Table

Seasonal plates shaped by coast, orchard, and hive.

At Foragers, the kitchen moves with the same rhythm as the orchard. What arrives on your table begins in field and forest, in hive and harvest.

The Philosophy

Great food does not compete with its ingredients — it reveals them

We believe great food does not compete with its ingredients. It reveals them.

Our culinary approach draws from the agricultural richness of the Sunshine Coast: local fisheries, small farms, foraged elements, and estate-grown honey and fruit. Dishes are built around balance — brightness against depth, texture against softness, salt against floral lift.

Mead is not simply served alongside the food. It informs it. You may find estate honey folded into a vinaigrette, a barrel-aged mead reduced into sauce, or orchard fruit layered through a dessert. What is poured in your glass may echo on your plate.

This is not novelty. It is continuity.

The Menu

Small plates for sharing, menus that follow the seasons

Our menu shifts frequently to reflect what is at its peak.

Expect small plates designed for sharing, alongside composed dishes that carry subtle multicultural influence — shaped by our team's experience in fine dining kitchens across Canada and beyond.

Seafood arrives fresh from coastal waters. Produce follows the cadence of nearby farms. Root vegetables, orchard fruit, and wild botanicals appear as the seasons change.

Each dish is built with intention, and with respect for what came before it.

Friday and Saturday evenings feature our full seasonal menu. Due to the nature of our preparation and sourcing, substitutions may be limited during peak service.

The Space

Above the orchard — a place to slow down and be present

The lounge sits above the orchard, surrounded by forest and coastal light.

Inside, natural textures meet modern comfort in an intimate setting designed for conversation. Outside, our covered patio offers warmth from the fireplace and views across the trees.

We have created a space that invites you to slow down — to be present with your table, your glass, and the people around you.

Hospitality Without Gratuity

Service that is warm, knowledgeable, and unhurried

Foragers operates as a tip-free establishment.

We believe hospitality should not depend on variable compensation. Our team is supported through living wages, equitable structure, and a culture built on mutual respect.

If a gratuity is left behind, it is donated to a local charity.

Service here is intentional — warm, knowledgeable, and unhurried.

A Quiet Adult Atmosphere

Designed for conversation, presence, and shared meals

Foragers is designed as an adult dining environment.

As a licensed alcohol manufacturing business, our lounge and patio are intended for guests 19 years and older. The space is crafted for conversation, focus, and the enjoyment of food and drink without distraction.

We ask that pets remain outside the dining areas, with the exception of service animals.

This is a place for gathering — thoughtfully and intentionally.

Come for the place. Stay for the moment.

Reservations are recommended, particularly for weekend evenings. Whether you join us for a tasting flight and small plates, or a full seasonal menu experience, we invite you to settle in.
