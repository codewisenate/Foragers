The Pour

Honey, fruit, and time in every glass.

Our meads are shaped first by blossom and harvest, and then by patience. Each expression begins with estate honey, guided fermentation, and careful maturation.

Our Approach

Mead treated with the same respect as fine wine

At Foragers, mead is treated with the same respect as fine wine and the same clarity as thoughtful cuisine.

We ferment small batches to preserve nuance and expression. Estate honey provides structure and aromatic complexity. When fruit is introduced — whether orchard apple, raspberry, peach, or blackcurrant — it is chosen for balance, not sweetness.

Fermentation is temperature-controlled to protect delicate floral notes. Some meads mature in stainless steel to retain brightness and clarity. Others rest in oak barrels in our lower cellar, developing depth, texture, and subtle tannin.

Intervention is minimal. Precision is intentional.

Current Expressions

Three styles — each shaped by a different expression of this place

Traditional Mead

Structured, nuanced, and reflective of terroir 11–14% ABV  ·  750 ml

Our traditional table meads focus on the expression of honey itself. Wildflower blossom, orchard bloom, and seasonal nectar create natural layers of floral, citrus, herbal, and mineral notes. Some vintages are stainless-aged for purity and lift. Others are barrel-aged to introduce quiet structure and gentle spice. Each vintage reflects the harvest year — subtle variations are expected and welcomed.

Session Mead

Bright, sparkling, and designed for ease 5–7% ABV  ·  Lightly sparkling

Our session meads showcase seasonal fruit layered over estate honey. Peach brings softness and stone-fruit aromatics. Raspberry introduces brightness and lift. Blackcurrant adds depth and coastal tartness. Carbonation is gentle, designed to refresh without overpowering the natural texture of honey. Served chilled, they are meant for long afternoons and shared plates.

Cyser

Where orchard meets hive ~7% ABV  ·  355 ml cans

Our cyser blends estate honey with heritage orchard apples, fermented together to create balance between floral sweetness and crisp acidity. Cyser carries notes of fresh apple skin, honeyed citrus, and subtle spice shaped by the orchard's character. It is coastal in nature — clean, structured, and quietly complex.

Vintage & Seasonality

Each release tied to a season — a moment captured in fermentation

We do not chase consistency at the expense of expression.

Honey composition shifts with bloom. Apple acidity varies with weather. Even subtle changes in temperature can influence fermentation character.

Rather than mask these differences, we allow them to inform the final result. Each release is tied to a season — a moment captured in fermentation and glass.

Pairing & Presence

Crafted to complement the kitchen

Our meads are crafted to complement the kitchen.

Traditional meads pair beautifully with seafood, aged cheeses, and roasted root vegetables. Session meads lift lighter fare and shared plates. Cyser bridges orchard fruit and savoury dishes alike.

In the lounge, you may find our meads incorporated directly into reductions, vinaigrettes, and desserts — creating continuity from glass to plate.

A Note on Sweetness

Clarity, lift, and restraint — not heaviness

Mead carries a long history of being misunderstood as uniformly sweet.

At Foragers, sweetness is measured and deliberate. Residual sugar is balanced with acidity, tannin, and structure. Many of our expressions finish dry or off-dry, allowing honey's complexity to emerge without heaviness.

The result is clarity, lift, and restraint.
