Dining lounge

Our dining lounge offers an intimate indoor dining lounge offering our seasonal menu and a covered patio overlooking the orchard.

For larger groups or special arrangements, please contact us directly at info@foragersmead.ca.

Seating & Timing

To ensure a relaxed and enjoyable experience for all guests:

  • Reservations are held for 15 minutes past the scheduled time.
  • Tables are allotted approximately 2 hours during peak service.
  • Walk-ins are welcome as space allows.

If you are running late, please contact us so we may accommodate you where possible.

Patio

The patio is open during the same hours as the dining lounge. Walk-ins are welcome; reservations are not required, though larger groups may require special arrangements.

Tasting Experiences

Guided flights of our current releases, in the dining lounge or at the tasting bar

Guests are invited to enjoy guided tasting flights of our current releases in the dining lounge or at the tasting bar.

Tastings are available during regular service hours and may be reserved in advance or enjoyed as space allows.

Guests must be 19 years of age or older and may be asked to present two pieces of valid identification.

Group Bookings

Private gatherings, celebratory dinners and larger parties

For private gatherings, celebratory dinners or larger parties, we offer limited group booking opportunities based on availability.

Our team can help arrange a relaxed and memorable experience, whether you are planning an intimate celebration, a shared tasting or a larger dinner with friends and family. Group bookings may include curated menus and guided mead selections designed to reflect the season and the estate.

Due to the size and nature of our dining space, advance planning is recommended.

Please inquire directly for details regarding curated menus and estate experiences at info@foragersmead.ca.

A Thoughtful Dining Environment

An adult space, designed for presence and shared meals

Foragers is designed as an adult dining space.

As a licensed alcohol manufacturing facility, guests must be 19 years or older to dine in the dining lounge and patio areas.

We kindly ask that guests move thoughtfully through the property, which remains an active orchard and apiary.

Service animals are welcome. Pets must remain outside dining areas.

Good hospitality is quietly intentional

Our Hospitality Model

Warm, attentive, unhurried and tip-free hospitality

Foragers operates as a tip-free establishment.

Our team is supported through living wages and professional compensation. If gratuities are left behind, they are donated to a local charity.

Hospitality here is warm, attentive and unhurried. Designed to allow the evening to unfold naturally.
