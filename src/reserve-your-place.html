In the dining lounge

Dining offers an intimate dining lounge for guests 13 years and older. The lounge features our seasonal menu with views overlooking the orchard.

Seating & Timing

To ensure a relaxed and enjoyable experience for all guests:

  • Reservations are held for 15 minutes past the scheduled time — if you are running late, please contact us so we may accommodate you where possible.
  • Tables are allotted approximately 2 hours during peak service.
  • Walk-ins are welcome as space allows.
  • Larger groups can often be accomodated with prior arrangement.

Outdoor Dining

Enjoy a meal on the covered patio

Our patio is open the same hours as the dining lounge. Settle in beside the stone fireplace and enjoy a relaxed meal with views of the orchard and apiary.

Reservations are not required, and seating is available on a walk-in basis. Larger groups may need to make special arrangements in advance.

While our bees typically go about their work without concern, Foragers is located on an active apiary. Guests with severe bee allergies who are dining on the patio should take appropriate precautions.

Group Bookings

Celebratory dinners and larger parties

We offer a limited number of group bookings of 7 or more throughout the season, depending on availability.

Our team can help arrange a relaxed and memorable experience, whether you are planning an intimate celebration, a shared tasting or a larger dinner with friends and family.

Due to the size and nature of our dining space, advance planning is recommended.

Our Hospitality Model

Warm, attentive, thoughtful hospitality

Foragers is a tip-free establishment.

Our team is supported through fair, professional compensation that reflects the care and skill they bring to each guest experience.

This approach allows hospitality to be offered with intention from the start, without expectation or additional transaction at the table.

We thank you for helping us maintain a space rooted in respect, for land, for craft, and for one another.
